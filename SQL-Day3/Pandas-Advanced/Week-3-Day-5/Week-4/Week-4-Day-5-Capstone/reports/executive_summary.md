# Executive Summary: Brazilian E-Commerce Sales Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes Brazilian e-commerce data from the Olist dataset to understand sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, payment preferences, product category performance, and customer satisfaction. Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn, and Power BI were used for data analysis and visualization.

## Key Findings

- **Sales Performance:** Total product sales were approximately R$13.59 million. November 2017 recorded the highest monthly product sales, at approximately R$1.01 million.
- **Product Categories:** Health & Beauty generated approximately R$1.26 million in sales, followed by Watches & Gifts at R$1.21 million and Bed, Bath & Table at R$1.04 million.
- **Customer Retention:** Approximately 3% of customers with delivered orders made repeat purchases, indicating an opportunity to improve customer retention.
- **Payment Preferences:** Credit cards were the most frequently used payment method, with 76,795 payment records.
- **Customer Satisfaction:** The average review rating was 4.09 out of 5.

## Business Recommendations

1. **Improve Customer Retention:** Introduce loyalty rewards, personalized offers, and follow-up campaigns to encourage customers to make repeat purchases.

2. **Optimize Product Category Strategies:** Prioritize inventory planning and targeted marketing for high-performing categories such as Health & Beauty and Watches & Gifts, while evaluating lower-performing categories for improvement opportunities.

3. **Improve Delivery and Customer Experience:** Monitor delivery delays, investigate recurring delivery issues, and use customer reviews to identify areas where service quality can be improved.

## Conclusion

The analysis highlights strong product sales, clear customer payment preferences, and opportunities to improve repeat purchasing and customer experience. The Power BI dashboard provides a visual overview of key business metrics to support data-driven decision-making.

## Limitations

The analysis uses historical data. The final months contain substantially fewer recorded orders, which may indicate incomplete data. Therefore, recent-month trends should be interpreted cautiously.
